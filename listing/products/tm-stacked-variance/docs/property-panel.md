## Scenarios and comparisons

Choose whether scenarios come from measures or dimension values. Set the comparison direction to left to right, outwards from a base scenario, or right to left. Use comparison pairs or select a preset. You can also set percentage precision, bridge style and shape, leader lines, direction markers, and callout words.

## Appearance

Set segment label content and placement, legend position, axis visibility, total labels, and tooltips. Choose how increases affect callout color. A tooltip can show up to three expression rows.
