order: 30
---

Select a segment to filter the Qlik app. Scenario selection is optional and only available when scenarios come from dimension values. Enable it in Scenarios & comparisons.

Tab to the chart. Use the arrow keys, Home, and End to move focus. Press Enter or Space to activate the focused chart item. Press Escape to cancel an active selection. Tab passes focus back to the host.
