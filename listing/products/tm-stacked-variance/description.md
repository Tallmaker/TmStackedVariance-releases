Tm Stacked Variance shows several scenarios as stacked columns inside your Qlik app. Each column carries its net total, and bridges with callouts show how much and in which direction each scenario differs from the next. Gains and losses stack on both sides of the line, so negative values stay honest. Click a segment to filter the rest of the sheet. Finance and planning teams use it to explain a gap to plan in one picture. It works best with up to eight segments per scenario.
